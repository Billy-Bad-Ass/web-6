Questions

Before you buy

What exactly do I get?

A PDF in both A4 and US Letter, in one download. Designed to be printed in black and white on ordinary paper and still be readable.

Do I need an account?

No. Payment goes through Stripe and the download link is sent to the email address you enter at checkout.

My download link stopped working.

Links expire for security, not to make you buy again. Email support@bbanetwork.org from the address you bought with and you will get a fresh one.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If it did not help, say so within 30 days and you will get your money back. You do not have to send the file back — you cannot un-read it, and pretending otherwise would be theatre.
